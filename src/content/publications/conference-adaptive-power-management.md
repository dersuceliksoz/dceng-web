An adaptive, state-tuned power management system for a series hybrid electric tracked
vehicle, coordinating battery and generator-set power through a hierarchical control
architecture. The approach was verified using Model-in-the-Loop and Driver-in-the-Loop
simulations under varying operating conditions and power demands.
