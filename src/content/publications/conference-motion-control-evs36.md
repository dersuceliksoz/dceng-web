Development of a mobility control system for high-speed series-hybrid electric tracked
vehicles with independently driven tracks. The work covers powertrain modeling and
dynamic behaviour analysis, and proposes a robust closed-loop control system for
precise, stable off-road operation across a range of driving conditions, with particular
attention to steering and pivoting manoeuvres.
