A steering-controlled braking and maneuvering system for electric tracked vehicles, designed
to improve maneuverability during normal operation and provide emergency steering capability
in the event of a main power system failure. The system selectively applies braking to the
desired track by distributing brake hydraulic pressure based on steering or brake inputs.
