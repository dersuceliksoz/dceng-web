This article presents a nonlinear state estimator designed to remain stable and
well-conditioned when measurements arrive intermittently. Conditions for bounded
estimation error are derived, and a tuning procedure trades estimator responsiveness
against noise sensitivity in a transparent way.

The approach is evaluated against logged sensor data with realistic dropout patterns,
and compared to standard filtering baselines under the same conditions.
