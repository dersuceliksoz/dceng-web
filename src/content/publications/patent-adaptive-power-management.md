A power management system for armoured series-hybrid vehicles, comprising input
pre-processing, state-machine boundary determination, state detection, and generator
drive control. The system distributes power between the battery and generator by
monitoring driver demand, system voltage, battery charge/discharge current limits,
battery temperature, and generator power limits, protecting battery integrity while
meeting instantaneous power demand.
