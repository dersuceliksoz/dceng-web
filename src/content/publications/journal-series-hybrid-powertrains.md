Development of a mobility control system for high-speed series-hybrid electric tracked
vehicles with independent traction motors. The work covers powertrain modeling, dynamic
behaviour analysis, and a closed-loop control system designed to ensure stable off-road
operation through demanding manoeuvres such as steering and pivoting. Performance is
validated through both offline simulation and real-time testing, showing improved
response for heavy-duty and defense applications.
