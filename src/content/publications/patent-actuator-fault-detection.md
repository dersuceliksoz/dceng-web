This patent describes a model-based method for detecting and isolating faults in
electromechanical actuators. A reference model predicts nominal response, and structured
residuals between predicted and measured behaviour are evaluated to flag and classify
fault conditions without interrupting normal operation.

The method is intended to run alongside the primary controller with low computational
overhead and a tunable sensitivity / false-alarm trade-off.
