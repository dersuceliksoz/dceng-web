A harmonic balance formulation for predicting the forced response of nonlinear structural
systems subjected to simultaneous excitation at multiple frequencies. The method extends
conventional single-frequency harmonic balance analysis to multi-tone loading, enabling
more realistic prediction of resonance and nonlinear interaction effects in structural
dynamics applications.
