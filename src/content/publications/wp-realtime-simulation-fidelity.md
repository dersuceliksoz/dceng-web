Higher model fidelity is not always better. This white paper offers a framework for
selecting the right level of detail for a real-time simulator, driven by which dynamics
actually influence the controller's decisions within its bandwidth.

It covers solver selection under hard real-time constraints, the trade-off between fixed
and variable step methods, and a checklist for confirming that simplifications do not
mask the failure modes a validation campaign is meant to expose.
